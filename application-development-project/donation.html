Support the Penang Heritage Trust (PHT)

Preserving George Town's UNESCO Legacy Since 1986

The Penang Heritage Trust (PHT) is a non-profit organisation and registered charity. We initiated the campaign that secured George Town's UNESCO World Heritage Site status in 2008, and we continue to champion the preservation of Penang's cultural and historical fabric today.

🏛️ Our Role in Heritage Protection


  • Key Milestone: Successfully achieved UNESCO Listing for George Town in 2008 after a 10-year campaign.

  • Governance: We are run by volunteer council members with dedicated full-time staff.

  • Advocacy: We work directly with government, media, and stakeholders to protect heritage and are the vital voice that "makes a stand" on crucial issues.

  • Community: We organise frequent site visits, talks, workshops, and events for our members.

  • Guidance: We provide information and advice to the public regarding repairs, research, and threats to heritage properties.

📢 Your Donation Makes a Difference


Facing growing urban development, the PHT must be strengthened to continue its advocacy and public service. Your contribution ensures we can preserve Penang's heritage for future generations.
